---
name: Churuchubu palette direction
description: Shared visual language for the Churuchubu fan site and related surfaces.
---

The shared design direction is a neutral-first interface: white, ink black, and gray carry most surfaces, text, borders, and secondary actions. Rose-pink is an accent reserved for important actions and states such as ticket purchase, selected navigation, live dates, new content, and critical notices. Member colors remain scoped to member-related UI.

**Why:** The site needs to retain its cute identity while gaining hierarchy, readability, trust, and a more premium feel.

**How to apply:** Prefer neutral tokens for default UI. Use the pink accent only when the user needs to notice, select, or act on something important; do not use it as a blanket icon, border, or label color.
